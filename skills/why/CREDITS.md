# Credits

Some ideas come from the `why` skill in Cursor's [pstack](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/main/pstack) plugin (MIT): one investigator per evidence source running in parallel, recording empty searches as results, keeping contradictions instead of a tidy story, and testing the user's hypothesis instead of confirming it. This skill is written independently and starts from a question about the product or a decision rather than from a piece of code; pstack is not a runtime dependency.
